What this establishes here

Training used an exact differentiable simulator and supervised future-answer labels. The sender is a stationary linear trace memory; the receiver is a small tanh network. No biological spike waveform, learned physical law or language-model replacement is established.

The matched recurrent receiver has 941 parameters against adaptive's 944, about 5% fewer neural MACs and three more persistent values. Adaptive commands twelve coefficients per read, whereas a fixed schedule can be shared beforehand. This is a prediction result under comparable resources, not a communication or energy win.

The adaptive head also changes the receiver's nonlinear computation through its own gate command. This comparison measures the implemented interface as a whole; it does not isolate every benefit of physical gate placement from that additional computation.

The read changes memory without mathematically erasing a dimension at η = 0.25. Erasure is a separate control. The original ecg.json feedback accident, AnttisBrain2's moons and BrainAsInverseModeler supply the question's history; their equations remain distinct.
