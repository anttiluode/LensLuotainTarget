WHAT WE CAN SAY

A learned interface to retained history.

What is actually matched

Every control has the same recurrent network and internal gate-and-threshold head, even when that head does not control the plant. Each gets 1,252 parameters and 7,376 counted neural MACs per episode. Sender and receiver retain 28 values.

Adaptive commands send thirteen coefficients per read; fixed schedules can be shared in advance. Counting excludes nonlinearities and plant work. Equal counts do not guarantee equal effective capacity.

Where this question came from

The original ecg.json feedback accident, the moons in AnttisBrain2 and Varjoluotain's mask put observation geometry inside the problem. Kuulustelu made the threshold's role experimentally explicit. Here the commands are learned.

This remains a simulation with known training dynamics, correlated non-Gaussian histories and an externally controllable threshold. The invertible write moves memory. It does not prove biological spiking, a new neuron or practical AI superiority.
